Removes quality progression and applies the highest available quality tier's bonuses
to factory entities and player equipment: legendary by default, or a higher
tier supplied by another mod. Enemies, asteroids and world hazards retain normal stats.

Requires **Factorio 2.1** with **Quality enabled**. Space Age is optional.

* Hides quality badges, selectors and quality unlocks; removes quality effects from modules.
* Hides unused quality modules and their research. Modules required by useful crafting
  recipes remain available as inert ingredients with their original costs and research.
  Recycling and module-only recipes do not trigger retention.
* Preserves existing item quality identities. Items with different original qualities
  remain separate stacks, even though their quality bonuses are identical.
* Some world entities added by other mods may require additional exclusions.

Releases are validated in headless Factorio with Quality alone and with Space Age,
including player, crafting and world-stat regression checks.
